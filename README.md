## 📦 Flagship Project — FulfillFlow

### Warehouse Fulfillment Operations Platform

**FulfillFlow** is a full-stack warehouse operations platform that models the complete journey of an e-commerce order — from picking and SKU verification through packing, staging, courier dispatch and shipment tracking.

It was designed around real operational problems such as **priority orders missing deadlines, inventory movement between warehouses, incorrect SKU fulfillment, misplaced staged packages and exceptions being forgotten.**

**Built with**

`React` · `FastAPI` · `Python` · `SQLAlchemy` · `SQLite` · `Recharts` · `REST APIs`

### ✨ Highlights

⚡ Real-time Operations Command Center  
🔍 SKU verification to reduce picking errors  
🏭 Multi-warehouse inventory & stock transfers  
📦 Picking → Packing → Staging → Dispatch workflow  
🚚 Courier selection & shipment tracking  
⚠️ Exception management  
📊 Operations analytics  
🕒 Complete order activity & audit trail
